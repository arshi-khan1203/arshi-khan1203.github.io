NovaCare Responsible AI Framework

AIML-510 Responsible Application of AI | Final integrated framework (Assignment 6.5)

This deck brings together a semester of work into one Responsible AI framework for NovaCare Health Systems, a fictional U.S. healthcare network. It covers the ethical principles statement, governance and roles, a 12-month implementation timeline, metrics, an AI lifecycle with disposition, risk guidelines (explainability, data bias, privacy and compliance), user education, long-term impacts, and best practices, with 13 prioritized APA references.

Download PPTXOpen PDFSupporting materials

What the framework covers

  • Seven principles, each with a measurable commitment and a named owner
  • Board-to-system governance with tiered approval rights
  • Seven RAI roles tied to the KPIs they answer for
  • 12-month phased timeline with checkpoints at months 3, 6, 9 and 12
  • Core and added metrics with thresholds and corrective actions
  • Eight-stage AI lifecycle from design to disposition
NovaCare Health Systems is a fictional organization created for this course case study.
